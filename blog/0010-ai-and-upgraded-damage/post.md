🤖 Smarter Bot AI & Upgraded Hero Damage

Two gameplay improvements to balance the game:

Smarter Bot Strategy: Bots now limit themselves for hero types instead of collecting everything. This creates more focused lineups and prevents random weak compositions.

Upgraded Heroes Deal More Damage: Each hero grade upgrade now grants +1 damage. This makes leveling heroes significantly more rewarding and helps bots catch up in late game.

Players now always start games with a one cost hero.
