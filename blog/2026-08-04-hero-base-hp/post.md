❤️ Hero base HP scales with cost

Introduced `getHeroBaseHp` utility that returns different base HP values based on character cost:

- Cost 1 heroes: 5 HP (Soldier, Archer, Priest, etc.)
- Cost 2 heroes: 8 HP (Swordsman, Wizard, Orc Rider, etc.)
- Cost 3 heroes: 12 HP (Elite Orc, Werebear, Greatsword Skeleton, etc.)

This makes higher-cost characters genuinely stronger right from the start.
